About White Lotus Landscaping

Owner-operated by Travis since 2019 — creative landscapes with calm, flow and lasting quality.

Hi, I’m Travis

I started White Lotus Landscaping in 2019 with a simple goal: craft outdoor spaces that feel calm and look incredible. As an owner-operator, I’m hands-on from first chat to final rake — design, build and finishing touches.

Landscaping is equal parts creativity and care. I focus on flow — how you move through a space — and on details like stone placement, planting palettes and edges that make everything feel intentional. Whether it’s a tranquil pondless waterfall, a full garden makeover, or practical paving & steps, I tailor each project to your site and lifestyle.

Based on the Gold Coast and working across the surrounding region, I’m proud to build lasting client relationships and spaces that get better with time.
